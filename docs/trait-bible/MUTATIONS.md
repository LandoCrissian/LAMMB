# Structural mutation specifications

Families are exclusive; neither mutation status nor unusual anatomy assigns a rarity tier. Fixed base replacements are representable by the existing engine. Conditional face/garment sources remain a production blocker. Horns are mutation-only.

## Skeletal

Stable trait: `lammb-mutations-skeletal`; PROPOSED.

Replace face, muzzle and visible neck with a coherent sheep skull and vertebral structure. Lateral ears and continuous wool remain.

Preserve: One coherent sheep muzzle and nose axis; Two primary eyes with meaningful eyelids; One low lateral ear pair with intact roots; Continuous recognizable curl silhouette at crown and temples; Short sheep neck and shoulder bust.

Wool: Retain species curl geometry. Any family-specific fiber contact/occlusion variant must be explicitly sourced and reviewed.

Eyes/expression: Sockets, cheek bones, nasal opening and one dental arcade remain coherent. Closed/limited mouth expressions only; expression assets need skeletal-specific geometry.

Clothing/accessories: Use declared compatibility constraints plus explicitly aligned anatomy-family variants for collars, eyes and eyewear.

Material: Bone and Wool. Effects: Restrained CCFF00 contact accents or drips; no decoration-based grail claim.

Forbidden: Two structural families on one specimen; Unregistered asset substitutions that erase selected visible trait differences; Rabbit-like ear structure or human-skull proportions; Face-obscuring effects, crowns or random duplicated anatomy.
