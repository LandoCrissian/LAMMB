It is ready for owner specification review. It does **not** approve references,
source artwork, final frequencies, a Genesis Base, grail classifications or NFT

## Species and composition standard

LAMMB is an anthropomorphic sheep with a compact rounded muzzle, broad low lateral
ears, a large wool-heavy head and deliberate heavy-lidded personality. Keep one
coherent head, one nose axis, one ear pair, two expressive primary eyes and short
sheep neck/shoulders. Normal anatomy has no horns. Skeletons retain sheep skull
proportions, wool and lateral ears; horns and secondary eyes belong only to explicit
structural families. Avoid long rabbit ears, human jaw proportions and pasted
generic skulls.

The species turnaround should establish a compact wool-covered trunk, short
forelimbs and hind limbs, and sheep hoof forms rather than human hands/feet or
rabbit legs. These proportions inform collar/shoulder anatomy even when below the
PFP crop. Do not publish limb traits that are not visible in the bust. Skeletal
replacement follows the sheep muzzle, dental arcade, jaw hinges and cervical axis;
it is a whole registered anatomical structure, not a human skull pasted over skin.

The proposed shared frame is a three-quarter left-facing square bust, 3072×3072
master and 1024×1024 presentation. Reserve at least an 8% circle-crop inset for the
ear tips and wool crown. These are proposed production constraints rather than
measurements inferred from the small reference crops. Select and approve a clean
identity/anatomy sheet before producing the aligned source pack. Every production
variant must preserve the same pose, lens, landmarks and light direction.

Wool must form deliberate curl clusters with continuous crown/temple coverage and
visible fiber roots. Do not replace wool with noise, melted loops or a flat helmet.
Eyes need purposeful lids, consistent pupils and controlled catchlights. Expressions
must move the sheep's actual anatomy; no pasted human mouths or random duplicate
teeth. A gold dental accent sits within one organized visible dental arcade.

Black clothing uses clean seams and purposeful folds. Cotton, wool, graphite metal,
bone, basalt, quartz, bark and negative space must have distinct surface behavior.
Gold and silver are accents. Radioactive CCFF00 emission and drips remain bounded;
they cannot conceal expression, ear roots or species identity. Pixel corruption is
geometric block displacement with a versioned mask, not rainbow material, blur or
JPEG damage. Six system names are established; new coverage ranges remain proposals.

No crowns or crown emblems, Colorado signage, borrowed NFT template anatomy,
invented lettering or random accessories. Exact `5280` typography is a separately
reviewed deterministic source, without a comma. Do not reproduce all accessories
from the owner-supplied skeletal portrait by default.

## Frequencies and uniqueness

Each category's proposed totals sum to exactly 5280. All approved counts are null.
Exact categories subtract each grail's contribution before passing ORDINARY_ONLY
quotas to the constructor; grails remain included in final totals. Multi Eye and
Observer Array have ordinary quotas zero. No metadata tier or proprietary ranking
is introduced. Owners must approve final frequencies separately from this design.

Weighted targets are integer selection weights, conditioned on compatibility and
logical uniqueness. They are not promises of exact final counts. The frequency
report exposes all differences, including restricted visible dental accents.
If the owner requires exact counts across dependent expression/accessory families,
feasibility must be demonstrated with the existing bounded constructor or a
separately reviewed scheduling extension; do not relax constraints or silently
change weights. The successful simulation proves this disclosed policy only.

Logical fingerprints bind selected identity traits and effective source IDs, not
token numbers, grail labels or weights. No label-only trait may manufacture visual
uniqueness. The 5280 logical identities still require decoded image comparison,
perceptual duplicate review and owner anatomy QA before they become approved art.

Format/check only changed files with the existing Prettier. Compare the two
generated bundle files byte-for-byte; do not compare elapsed times or absolute
paths. Fresh output directories prevent overwrites. The simulation adapter creates
explicit DEVELOPMENT_ONLY descriptor bytes in memory, not PNGs or approved sources.
Its artifacts and metadata previews remain under ignored `artifacts/generated/`.

Existing CI is unchanged. It may run unrelated website/browser suites remotely;
those suites are not repeated locally and their screenshot artifacts are not
required to review this collection specification. No merge or deployment is
authorized. Stop for owner specification and artwork-plan review.
