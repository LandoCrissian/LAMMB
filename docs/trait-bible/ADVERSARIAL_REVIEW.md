## Diversity assessment

63 values across nine categories are numerically sufficient for 5280 unique logical
identities, but that is not a visual-diversity certificate. The normal family has
4600 specimens (87.12%); one base pose/frame, largely similar curl geometry and dark
streetwear dominate it. Eight eye entries include seven mainly color variations
and one reserved orbital array. Most wool entries change color or surface treatment;
Rolled Locks has the clearest separate shape. Six expressions are useful, but their
gaze, brow and mouth differences must survive thumbnail reduction.

Baseline projections measured from all specimens:

| Retained distinctions                                                         | Distinct configurations | Specimens in repeated groups | Largest group |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------: | ---------------------------: | ------------: |
| All character traits; omit background and pixel level                         |                    4059 |                         2000 |             9 |
| Also omit eye color                                                           |                    2340 |                         3895 |            32 |
| Family, expression, clothing, broad wool shape and headwear/eyewear/equipment |                     854 |                         4878 |           195 |

The last projection groups clustered curls together and omits wool color/material,
eye color, minor jewelry/tag/dental, background and corruption. It is deliberately
coarse. Effects and color can be compelling; these groups are **not identical image
claims, perceptual duplicate detections or rarity scores**. No production images
exist to measure those. The nine-member first group shares normal anatomy, ivory
wool, radioactive eyes, heavy lids, hoodie and no accessories; only the environment
and corruption selection distinguishes the logical characters. Indices are in the
evidence for a future art review, not token assignments.

Recommended material improvements, pending owner decisions:

1. Keep the eight wool slots initially, but require at least three clearly different
   curl silhouettes: compact crown, broad/offset crown and rolled locks. Decide
   which existing color definitions carry the first two shapes; do not add another
   color merely to raise the trait count. Check ear visibility and hat contacts
   before approving the change. No new geometry or counts are approved here.
2. Separate expression ownership from eye-color ownership. Side Eye must move both
   pupils together; Defiant needs a distinct brow/mouth. Require these differences
   to read at 64px/128px in the future collection asset review. Do not enlarge dental
   accents to fake diversity.
3. Compare hoodie/bomber/lab-jacket/coat silhouettes at thumbnail size. A long-coat
   hem outside a bust crop contributes nothing; its visible collar must differ.
4. Review the projected repeated groups before raising corruption coverage. More
   damage is not a replacement for better anatomy/expression. Do not reject logical
   identities solely because a coarse projection groups them together.

## Accessory model assessment

The existing engine selects one accessory configuration. Twelve values include
None and only one two-facet bundle, Cap and Shades. In the baseline, 1453 specimens
have no accessory facets, 3440 have one and 387 have two; the only two-facet pairing
is Headwear + Eyewear. Hat + ear tag, hat + chain, glasses + dental accent, or chain +
tag cannot coexist. A gold tooth consumes the entire accessory slot.

This constrains personality and exposes a hidden production tradeoff: independent
accessory axes would increase placement/occlusion work, not just combinations.
The recommended first option is **two or three deliberate bundles** within the
existing category, such as cap + 5280 tag, beanie + short chain, and shades + visible
gold tooth. Reallocate existing configuration counts only after owner approval,
retain None, require family/mouth/collar clearance and emit only the actual facets.
Do not build a Cartesian catalog of every prop pairing. Independent axes require
a separately reviewed cardinality/quota change; this PR does not implement it.

## Mutation assessment

Six ordinary structural families and the reserved five-eye family offer meaningful
material diversity. Their exact totals are 220 skeletal, 180 cybernetic, 100 magma,
72 crystalline, 64 botanical, 43 Void and one Multi Eye. One archetype per family is
a useful coherent start, but family names alone do not prove varied silhouettes.

- Skeletal expression restrictions protect geometry but narrow mood variety; prove
  the compatible Defiant dental source rather than assuming the baseline zero is
  intentional scarcity. Preserve sheep skull/ears/wool, not a human skull overlay.
- Cybernetic requires fitted mechanisms and exclusion of headset intersections;
  avoid making all 180 faces the same cheek plate with different colored irises.
- Magma has only three wool and three clothing choices. Controlled basalt horn
  profiles and heat paths could improve it, but require explicit source identities
  and approval rather than unrecorded procedural variation.
- Crystalline needs real optical integration; opaque face layers over quartz would
  falsely advertise the material. The crystal/gold exclusion is intentional.
- Botanical must change structure, not add leaves to normal skin. Beanie clearance
  and coat/neck occlusion need actual artwork review.
- Void permits three light wool values and three closed-mouth expressions. Negative
  space must still retain eyes, ear roots and a continuous wool island.
- Multi Eye is one fixed reserved composition, not a seven-seed variety axis. Its
  24 catalog family bindings include hypothetical alternatives that the current
  one-reservation policy will not use. A compiler must derive the actual frozen
  inventory before anyone commissions every catalog possibility.

Do not increase mutation totals simply to create apparent rarity. Owner may choose
small, silhouette-distinct family subtypes after a family pilot, but the current
catalog cannot represent those new values until reviewed.

## Artwork feasibility

**Included ImageGen is a plausible source-art tool, not a proven 5280-production
system.** The built-in tool is exposed in this session. The current read-only
account snapshot reports ordinary usage allowed, 36% of the visible weekly Codex
window used (64% remaining), no purchased credits, balance zero, unlimited false.
It exposes no image-call budget or throughput guarantee. This snapshot can change.

[Official OpenAI pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing) confirms image
generation shares general included limits, typically consumes them faster, and
uses credits after exhaustion. API-key pricing is separate. [Official ImageGen
guidance](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/image-generation) supports references for
content/style/layout; it does not establish registered layer output or unlimited
batch capacity. The current documentation names a model, but recovered Task 005D
records do not establish that historical model/version or a reproducible seed.
No API keys, purchases, resets, new subscriptions, model downloads or ImageGen
calls occurred in this review. Recheck included access before any later pilot and
stop on cost uncertainty; no paid fallback is authorized.

Task 005D proves built-in generation of cinematic website direction (front master
1024×1536, no local GPU inference, recorded Sharp optimization), not aligned
collection layers. Preserved ART 005 quality evidence reports a 1254×1254 character
despite requesting 2048, nonuniform chartreuse, wrong facing direction, tight ear
margins and corruption into an ear fringe. It was not production approved. These
are concrete reasons not to promise native 3072, exact backgrounds or positional
obedience from prompts alone.

### Source inventory and alignment

Baseline 76 templates comprise 68 ImageGen/alignment proposals, four clear layers
and four deterministic-vector sources. 267 family bindings replace 39 generic
parent templates: `76 - 39 + 267 = 304` expanded source bindings, **not 343 files or
267 additional traits**. After the impossible dental row is removed, the analogous
count is **303** with 266 bindings. These are planning bindings, not unique files
or required ImageGen calls. Shared byte sources, deterministic masks, reserved-only
alternatives and curated scene overrides can reduce actual work; none is proven.

The family matrix does not resolve expression × eye gaze, wool × hat,
expression × tooth, or collar × chain contacts. For example, an eye source above
the expression source can overwrite Side Eye's pupil direction. Fixed family-only
bitmaps are inadequate for these dependencies. A production plan must select and
prove one of these contracts before source commissioning:

1. Expression owns lids, gaze, pupils and mouth geometry; eye color is a registered
   iris-mask/material operation. Dental accents use the selected expression's tooth
   mask, not a fixed tooth decal.
2. Alternatively, explicitly compile approved joint family/expression/eye/dental
   sources with matching display values and identity digests. Count that expanded
   inventory and verify aggregate quotas before approving it.

Likewise, register wool/hat contact regions and collar/jewelry anchors. Preserve
model outputs as immutable originals and store alignment, extraction masks and
every derivative hash separately. Reference-conditioned images still need measured
anchors; they are not interchangeable layers merely because they share a prompt.
The current constructor's fixed category replacement cannot resolve these joint
dependencies; a renderer/approved compiler contract is missing, not a reason to
duplicate the constructor.

### Resolution, crop, background and effects gates

- Treat 3072×3072 as a proposed final canvas, not proven native model detail. Record
  actual native dimensions. Owner must decide a supported native source standard
  after a pilot; deterministic fill/masking is valid, interpolation is not new detail.
- Register ear roots, eye centers, muzzle axis, crown, shoulders, collars and accessory
  anchors numerically in the shared frame. Check square and circular PFP crops;
  keep ears/crown/eyes inside the owner-approved safe region. No exact safe margins
  or landmark coordinate maps are approved yet.
- Exact CCFF00 is a deterministic background source. Extract edges without green
  halos and validate actual alpha/profile after decoding; declaration alone is not
  proof. No automatic AI recolor can certify exact background pixels.
- Corruption must operate on the assembled subject with a defined subject-alpha
  denominator and protected landmark masks, including secondary eyes. Coverage
  ranges do not themselves implement displacement or visual severity. Five proposed
  ImageGen effect sources cannot replace a reproducible subject-aware effect algorithm.
- Source-over compositing cannot infer quartz refraction, Void cutouts or correct
  neck occlusion. Family materials require approved integrated sources or a tested
  deterministic material/mask operation, not arbitrary layer ordering.

The PNG decoder/compositor is absent. Actual dimensions/alpha/profile are declared
in ingestion contracts, not independently decoded there. Source loading is capped
at 1 MiB/file and 64 MiB total. A decoded 3072 RGBA frame alone is 36 MiB; 303 such
buffers would be about 10.65 GiB before working copies. Those are arithmetic memory
estimates, not measured compressed sizes or a workstation benchmark. Stream a
measured per-specimen source set in a later renderer pilot; do not raise limits or
bypass RAM gates blindly. Pin decoder/codec versions and prove byte reproducibility.
