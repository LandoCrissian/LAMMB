# Wool silhouette specification — Task 015B

The owner authorized three silhouettes within the existing eight wool values. These are geometry specifications, not extra metadata values or approved artwork. Ratios are design requirements to test against the approved pilot frame.

## Ivory Curls

Stable ID: `lammb-wool-ivory`; profile: **COMPACT_CROWN**.

Low continuous crown of tight clustered curls; crown rise is approximately 18–24 percent of skull height. Center the top on the muzzle axis, without an offset crest or long hanging rolls.

Ears: Keep both original ear roots and the low lateral ear pair fixed to the approved anatomy anchors. Show both outer ear contours; maintain a headwear-to-ear gap of at least 2 percent of skull width. Wool sits behind the roots, never replaces an ear or covers the primary eye region.

Headwear: Beanie or cap configurations require registered contact masks; crown/temple curl islands and both ears remain visible. No hat may replace the selected wool color/material.

Preserved landmarks: One coherent sheep muzzle and nose axis; Two primary eyes with meaningful eyelids; One low lateral ear pair with intact roots; Continuous recognizable curl silhouette at crown and temples; Short sheep neck and shoulder bust.

Sources: asset-lammb-wool-ivory; family variants MISSING.

- Retain the existing stable wool ID, display name and material/color; geometry is part of this wool value, not a new public rarity attribute.
- Approve a lossless alpha wool source and crown/temple/ear masks for each supported anatomy family, with numerically registered skull/ear anchors.
- Approve every used wool/headwear contact derivative; demonstrate this silhouette at 64px and 128px and within square/circular crop.
- Record actual native dimensions and source/derivative hashes; never describe interpolation as new native detail.

Stable ID: `lammb-wool-ash`; profile: **BROAD_OFFSET_CROWN**.

Broad continuous crown with one deliberately offset upper lobe on viewer left; crown rise is approximately 30–40 percent of skull height and peak offset 10–15 percent of skull width. Widen the crown above the ears, not the muzzle or ear-root spacing.

Ears: Keep both original ear roots and the low lateral ear pair fixed to the approved anatomy anchors. Show both outer ear contours; maintain a headwear-to-ear gap of at least 2 percent of skull width. Wool sits behind the roots, never replaces an ear or covers the primary eye region.
