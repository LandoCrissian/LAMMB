## Integration decisions

The 720 change exposed three coupling errors: altitude schemas/presentation still used mint supply, historical pinned Trait Bible simulations were validated against the new cap, and development recipe hashes inherited the changed production config. The recovery branch injects separate 5280-foot heritage altitude, uses an explicitly archived development protocol/config for original simulations, and retains exact source approvals/goldens. Production construction still requires exactly 720; current recovery counters reject 721 and above. Neither archived proposal frequencies nor logical indices assign Genesis rarity or token IDs.

The full website remains PRE_ASCENT with mint unavailable. Updating the canonical constant alone does not implement an on-chain cap, eligibility or reveal. See [mint specification](../../packages/contracts/genesis-mint.spec.md).

Owner review sequence: inspect focused Chamber PR; inspect recovery diff relative to PR32 plus the PR33 commits; verify exact-head CI and source-preservation evidence; complete private independent recovery; approve a contract/mapping/metadata/host design. Resolve overlap deliberately when choosing which draft histories to adopt. No merge or registration is authorized by any PASS row.
