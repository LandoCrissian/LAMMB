# LAMMB universe art direction — working specification 0.1.0

These are provisional creative families for authored character development, not new Trait Bible IDs, rarity classes, collection allocations or utility promises. Supply remains 5280. Trait Bible v1.1.0, its compatibility rules, frequency proposals and six existing grail reservations remain unchanged. Owner approval of an artistic direction never registers its sources for production.

## Shared identity

Every family inherits the owner-approved compact sheep muzzle, low lateral ears, rooted curled wool, heavy-lidded green-eyed attitude and recognizable head proportions. Preserve the LAMMB personality: unimpressed, rebellious, slightly absurd and expressive. Cute appeal can come from soft wool, a cheeky expression and tactile materials; tougher appeal comes from posture, tailoring and structural materials. Neither warrants a new species, rabbit proportions, a monkey muzzle, human skull substitutions or a generic mascot.

Use deliberate studio lighting, material separation and waist-up PFP compositions. Head and ears should remain readable at 64px and 128px. Minor framing differences and intentional bottom-edge portrait cropping are acceptable artistic choices. The 2% silhouette guard and 8% inset remain diagnostic guidance. They are not automatic rejection thresholds or OpenSea requirements. No crowns, unnecessary lettering, random props, duplicate faces or floating anatomy.

Show an anatomically coherent stylized ovine skull/mandible, lower incisiform arcade and posterior cheek teeth; no upper anterior incisors or human collarbone structures. 3. **Refined Pixel Drip:** use approved front identity and Neon styling references. Localize chartreuse fragments/drips to wool and clothing while preserving the primary face and coherent material roots. Do not assume a TOUCH/BLEED coverage class without a reviewed effect mask. No new trait ID or silent metadata interpretation.

Maximum one ImageGen call per study, no automatic retry. Before each call require available RAM of at least 2048 MiB, current included allowance and no purchased-credit/API route. Preserve the generated native PNG without recompression, exact prompt, actual raster paths/hashes and tool-returned filename identifier. Model version, seed, identity weights, exact dimensions and pixel protection are not exposed as controllable guarantees by the built-in interface. A full-frame AI edit is not pixel-exact deterministic compositing.

Official documentation says built-in image generation uses general Codex included limits; API-key use follows API pricing. Current account evidence, not that general statement alone, is required before each call. Exact per-image debit is not exposed. [OpenAI pricing guidance](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing), [image input guidance](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/image-generation).

Ovine dental QA uses university anatomy evidence: sheep lack upper incisors and have a lower incisiform arcade, with grinding cheek teeth farther back. This is a constraint on the stylized interpretation, not a claim that speculative mutation tissue is veterinary anatomy. [New Mexico State sheep dentition](https://pubs.nmsu.edu/_b/B412/index.html).

The actual Stage 21 outputs are all native 1254×1254 PNGs. Magma Hoodie and Pixel Drip are promising owner-review studies. The skeletal attempt still fails ovine skull/dental direction despite improved upper-front-tooth and collarbone avoidance; preserve it as quarantined negative evidence. Three calls are complete, with no retry or production promotion.

## Collection diversity gaps

The recovered library has 23 principal art-direction approvals, two conditional directions and three rejected principal masters. Most of the later inventory consists of references, previews, masks and copies; file count is not character diversity. Stage 20 exported 19 byte-distinct candidates, not 19 proven collector-distinct configurations. Sixteen similarity-screened pairs need human review; front/smirk and front/irritated are particularly close.

- **Expressions:** irritated, tongue-out and skeptical interpretations need honest existing-value mapping or separately authorized specification work. Side Eye, Stoic, Amused and Defiant still need explicit premium sources. Strengthen expression differences without skull redesign or a generic human smile.
- **Poses:** front, left/right three-quarter, tilt and lean are useful authored families. True profiles and other body postures need new anatomy/contact sources. The crossed-forelimb study remains conditional; hoof construction needs review. A flip/warp is not a pose source.
- **Wool:** three demonstrated profiles cover compact ivory, broad Ash and rolled locks. The other existing color/material values lack registered sources, and Stage 3 proved that independent full-frame wool portraits are not interchangeable alpha layers.
- **Clothing:** black hoodies dominate. Bomber/beanie-chain is promising; the Neon jacket's exact Shell interpretation is unresolved. Lab Jacket, Utility Vest, Crewneck and Lab Coat need meaningful tailored source work. Do not count recolors as new silhouettes.
- **Mutations:** Skeletal and Void coverage are unresolved. Magma's new hoodie study conflicts with the current logical whitelist. Cybernetic, Crystalline and Botanical need anatomy-specific expression/wool/clothing contacts; accepted artwork alone cannot certify replacement anatomy. Multi Eye retains its existing reserved/ordinary-disabled status.
- **Effects/backgrounds:** localized corruption needs reviewed coverage, occlusion and source-family control. Existing neutral studio fields do not establish all flat or laboratory backgrounds. Energy should serve anatomy rather than hide it.
- **Special authored specimens:** unusual coherent silhouettes, material phenomena and personality can warrant curated full-frame treatment. Existing six grail reservations remain untouched; neither a singleton nor additional jewelry automatically makes exceptional art.

The logical engine can construct 5280 unique identities, but that does not demonstrate 5280 distinct finished artworks. Preserve frequencies while creating a reviewed source-coverage matrix. Owner selection, truthful complete trait vectors, source-specific recipes, visual duplicate review and measured pilot throughput precede manufacturing. No generation schedule, unlimited ImageGen access, token assignment or collection batch is authorized here.
