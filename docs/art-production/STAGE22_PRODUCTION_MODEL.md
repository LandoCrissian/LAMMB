## Pixel distribution and coverage plan

The observed Stage 21 frame has two deliberate areas: viewer-left crown/temple wool and viewer-right shoulder/sleeve cloth. Primary eyes, nose and muzzle remain recognizable. Some detached adjacent cubes and voxel-like fragments require a design interpretation. A region bounding box is not an effect mask; bright chartreuse segmentation also counts reflected light and cannot certify corruption.

Review four masks in one registered native coordinate system: subject silhouette, wool effect, garment effect and detached decorative particles. Keep particle coverage separate from damaged subject coverage; avoid double-counting wool/cloth overlap. Compute the subject-intersection union divided by the reviewed subject silhouette, and preserve uncertain boundaries rather than thresholding them away. Require owner review of 64px/128px previews and the mask before selecting a progression value. This task supplies annotated distribution references, not certified pixel masks.

Existing progression remains: NONE; TOUCH at most 3%; BLEED 3–8%; FRACTURE 8–15%; GLITCHED 15–25%; REALITY_FAILURE 25–40%. Their edge/material definitions also matter; percentages alone are insufficient. The shared endpoints (3, 8, 15 and 25) require an explicit interpretation when measurements land there. Optional/absent corruption is a genuine design choice. Localized wool-only, cloth-only and coordinated effects can create meaningful authored differences; extensive effects require coherent identity windows and are not the default LAMMB species.

## Hybrid manufacturing route

**A — Full-frame authored LAMMBs.** Preserve immutable native frames, exact artist/tool provenance and source-specific observed facets. Register pose/anatomy and complete truthful trait vectors before scheduling them. Do not count a single frame repeatedly under different metadata labels. ImageGen is creative authorship, not deterministic replay.

**B — Compatible deterministic composites.** Resolve only registered base/contact/mask recipes with exact historical output hashes, bounded alpha and outside-mask preservation. Stage 4/5/7 and merged Stage 17 establish specific ear-tag contacts; they do not establish arbitrary wool, hoodie, expression or pose interchangeability. New joints require independent registration and owner approval.

**C — Integrated special mutations.** Prefer authored anatomy-family frames and compatible family-specific derivatives. The Magma Hoodie is in this lane as an art-approved development direction, with current collection compatibility blocked. Structural anatomy replaces existing anatomy rather than stacking a second face. Existing six grail reservations, frequencies, supply 5280 and deterministic logical engine remain unchanged.

Use a source-coverage matrix against the existing logical simulation before production. Queue immutable source recipes, checkpoint rendered hashes and metadata receipts, quarantine missing/incompatible/rejected inputs, and reject byte-identical candidate outputs. Human review resolves perceptual near-duplicates and meaningful family coverage. No estimate of 5280 distinct finished images, mass throughput, ImageGen allowance or schedule follows from this small library.

The smallest next task is a no-generation Pixel Drip semantic coverage/mapping review, followed by an owner decision on the Magma Hoodie compatibility exception. Artwork priorities after separate authorization: a veterinary-grounded skeletal construction study; a Lab Jacket/Utility Vest silhouette pair with expressive biological faces; and source-specific Side Eye/Amused/Defiant studies spanning cute and intimidating appeal. Void recovery and new backgrounds follow identity/material coherence. No generation, collection assignment or production approval is authorized by this plan.
