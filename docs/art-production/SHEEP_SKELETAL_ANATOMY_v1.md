# Sheep skeletal anatomy — LAMMB design guide 0.1.0

This reference-backed design specification precedes any separately authorized skeletal study. It is not new artwork, veterinary advice, a production registration or an amendment to Trait Bible v1.1.0. Stage 12, 13 and 21 skeletal outputs stay rejected/quarantined. The approved biological LAMMB face supplies identity and expression; real sheep anatomy supplies the skeletal construction. Neither a filename nor an appealing render can override species QA.

## Anatomical evidence and permitted stylization

| Structure             | Real sheep / comparative evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | LAMMB interpretation and rejection gate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rostrum and jaw       | The ovine facial skeleton projects forward from the braincase; nasal, incisive and maxillary regions continue along the muzzle. The mandible has a long body, a toothless interval and a posterior ascending ramus, rather than a short human chin. The Sharri sheep study supplies lateral/dorsal/ventral skull and jaw landmarks; its breed measurements are not universal proportions. [Sharri sheep morphology, Figures 1–5](https://d-nb.info/1250492890/34). | Shorten this ovine construction deliberately to fit the approved compact cartoon muzzle, while retaining forward maxillary length and the sheep jaw curve. Do not simply expose a human skull under the wool. Use lateral and three-quarter checks before judging a frontal view. No invented numeric skull ratio.                                                                                    |
| Front dentition       | Sheep have no upper incisors; living animals bite against a dental pad. The adult lower front arcade comprises three pairs of incisors and a pair of incisiform canines: eight incisiform elements, not eight incisors plus two additional fangs. Cheek teeth lie farther back. [NMSU Guide B-412](https://pubs.nmsu.edu/_b/B412/index.html).                                                                                                                      | In a bare skull the dental pad is soft tissue, not an invented bony upper tooth row. Show a coherent narrow lower arcade and a toothless interval before posterior grinding teeth. Visible count depends on camera and occlusion: eight is the anatomical total, not a demand that eight be visible. Reject human front teeth, a human grin, detached dental blocks or fang-like lateral substitutes. |
| Orbits and cheekbones | Ruminant orbits have a complete bony boundary. The zygomatic region and posterior jaw articulation should be distinguished from the eye socket. Sheep-specific lateral skull figures establish their relationship to the rostrum; the university chapter also covers broader ungulate anatomy. [CVM head anatomy](https://open.lib.umn.edu/largeanimalanatomy/chapter/neck-head/), [Sharri sheep study](https://d-nb.info/1250492890/34).                          | Keep sheep-lateral socket placement while projecting the owner-approved primary eyes consistently into the PFP pose. Connect the zygomatic arch to the skull; do not create a second orbit from a cheek cavity. Heavy eyelids may survive as explicitly retained soft tissue, or be suggested by orbital brow geometry. Bone itself does not become a realistic eyelid.                               |
| Horns                 | Sheep horns have a bony core associated with the frontal region and a keratin sheath; horn presence varies. [Primary comparative horn-material study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6030630/), [museum Ovis aries cranium and mandible NH.0.173](https://www.horniman.ac.uk/object/NH.0.173/).                                                                                                                                                          | Horns are not required by a generic sheep skull or newly authorized for this study. Where the approved family actually requires them, establish paired frontal attachments, distinguish bone from sheath, and preserve ear/wool clearance. No detached horn props or crown.                                                                                                                           |
| Neck                  | A mammalian cervical column has seven vertebrae; atlas and axis organize the skull/neck connection. [University of Minnesota ungulate osteology](https://vanat.ahc.umn.edu/ungDissect/Lab08/xLab08.html).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Show a connected, pose-consistent neck entering the garment, with meaningful joints rather than stacked floating rings. Do not force all seven vertebrae into a small visible neckline. Root surviving wool remnants plausibly; wool is retained soft tissue, not a detached bone layer.                                                                                                              |
| Shoulders             | The forelimb connects through scapular and humeral anatomy; ungulates lack the human-style clavicular shoulder bridge. General ungulate references establish this boundary, not an exact upright cartoon sheep pose. [CVM thoracic limb](https://open.lib.umn.edu/largeanimalanatomy/chapter/thoracic-limb-forelimb/), [University of Maryland comparative ungulates](https://science.umd.edu/classroom/bsci338m/Lectures/Ungulates.html).                         | Anthropomorphic posture is deliberate stylization. Keep the upper torso inside an anatomically coherent hoodie unless a separately reviewed ovine thorax is available. Explicitly prohibit human clavicles and human-like exposed shoulder skeletons. A garment cannot excuse floating cervical anatomy.                                                                                              |

## Required next-study review

Before generation, the owner should review a side/three-quarter construction plan mapping the compact LAMMB muzzle onto the ovine maxilla, diastema, mandible and lateral orbit. Choose explicitly how much soft tissue survives. An expert reviewer should inspect jaw/teeth and neckline at native resolution, then evaluate personality and species readability at 128px and 64px. Reject human skull proportions, human front teeth, human clavicles, human-like exposed shoulders, second faces, floating bones or incoherent jaw articulation. Rendering quality cannot compensate for these defects.

No new skeletal image is authorized by this document. Reference links support factual anatomy; recommendations for cartoon compression, cloth coverage and artistic acceptance are our proposed design rules. Breed, age, sex, horn status and camera affect real specimens. Do not import a goat, horse or cow illustration as a sheep-specific proportion template.

References were inspected on 2026-10-11. The Minnesota book moved to `open.lib.umn.edu`; use those direct links. Some publisher full-text pages were unavailable; the horn claim is limited to the primary study's indexed abstract/excerpt. No third-party plates are copied into production artwork. University material includes noncommercial or educational-use restrictions; a reference citation does not license its illustration for NFT art. The owner-review package contains only our own schematic and preserved LAMMB QA images, with linked external plates.
