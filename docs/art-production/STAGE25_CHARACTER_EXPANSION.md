The expression is legible at 64px; only small lower dental tips appear and no human upper front row, duplicate tongue/eye or major artifact is observed. Fine left fringe is tight; no numerical semantic framing pass is claimed.

**Rolled Locks Evolution:** fibrous coils, roots, overlapping crown volume and lighting are coherent; the front-facing pose offers a potential family source. However, the dominant three front coils repeat the earlier three-quarter source's grammar at 64px/128px, and the unimpressed personality adds little novelty. It is **not selected as a production candidate**. The `NEEDS_REFINEMENT` class records this weak differentiation; no retry or automatic approval follows. The original approved Rolled Locks artwork/decision remains unchanged. A future study would need substantially different staggered/descending coil geometry within the existing wool specification, rather than another camera change.

**Lab Coat:** broad hoodless lapels, asymmetric fastening, oversized sleeves and coat torso expand the silhouette beyond black hoodie and Utility Vest. Wool/collar contact, fabric folds and button/pocket construction are coherent; no exposed human hands or extra limbs are observed. The head is smaller to show the garment but remains readable at 64px. Scientist personality is restrained, and the dark garment may read as a peacoat at thumbnail scale. Two small pen-like pocket inserts are authored details, not a new independent Accessory value. Owner review must judge the laboratory direction and character appeal.

All native images and 64/128/256 square/circular previews were inspected. Intentional waist-up bottom cropping is acceptable; circles trim outer clothing/fringe.
