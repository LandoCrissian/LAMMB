# Future high-resolution delivery plan — not executed

All four current proofs are genuinely **1254×1254**, byte-identical to their
historical native artwork. They meet the present bounded reconstruction contract
and read at 64/128/256 PFP sizes, while inherited ear/circular framing limitations
remain visible. They do not meet OpenSea's current media guidance to use images
at least 3000×3000. That guidance is separate from the JSON format and does not
make a larger raster evidence of improved artistic detail.
[Official OpenSea media guidance, reviewed 2026-10-12](https://docs.opensea.io/docs/media-and-traits)

## Proposed delivery target and evidence

Propose **3072×3072** for a future owner-reviewed delivery pilot, subject to real
source detail and measured budgets. No 3072px decode/render is authorized here.
The 1254px masters, source hashes, contacts, masks and current renderer limits
remain immutable. The current proof adapter still bounds each source to 4 MiB,
the aggregate to 8 MiB and dimensions/pixels to 1254/1254². Legacy ingestion's
1 MiB cap is unchanged.

First determine whether a genuinely higher-resolution original or reconstructible
authoring source exists. The four recovered PNGs contain only 1254px native
detail; no undiscovered larger master is assumed. A native re-render or newly
authored higher-detail source needs separate art authorization, identity/anatomy
QA and fresh provenance. Do not promise that ImageGen exposes a chosen native
resolution, identity lock or deterministic replay.

If no higher-detail authoring source can be obtained, present that limitation to
the owner. A separately approved deterministic delivery resample can be clearly
classified as a **derived raster**, never a new native master or invented detail.
It may change pixel dimensions but cannot substantiate a premium-detail claim.
Canvas enlargement with unchanged small artwork also does not improve detail or
solve missing ear anatomy. Neither operation occurs in this task.

## Required future validation

1. Preserve native masters and append derivative lineage, methods, codec versions
   and exact new hashes. New source/output hashes are outside the current four
   approved source bindings and require explicit derivative/source approval.
2. Assess actual face/eye/muzzle/wool detail at 1:1, clothing, ear-tag numeral
   fidelity, contact shadows, edges and lighting. Use 64/128/256 PFP and full-size
   review; do not judge quality by dimension alone.
3. Re-register contact geometry and masks for the new artwork. Do not scale a
   contact and claim the original source-pixel equality test still applies.
   Record any resampling and use a new reconstruction contract/output hash.
4. Introduce a narrow separate decoder-budget pilot only after source byte sizes
   and actual decoding/encoding RSS are measured. At 3072², one RGB buffer is
   exactly **27 MiB**, and one RGBA buffer **36 MiB**; these are lower-bound pixel
   allocations, not total process-memory estimates. Multiple copies, masks,
   compression buffers and library overhead must be measured.
5. Maintain a fresh >=2048 MiB available-RAM gate, CPU concurrency 1 initially,
   bounded source/aggregate bytes and decoded pixels, CRC/scanline/format checks,
   malformed/decompression-bomb rejection and deterministic codec tests. No
   unlimited cap increase, GPU inference or heavy local website workload.
6. Review collector appeal, visible trait correspondence and framing for each
   derivative. Source-specific definitions do not authorize arbitrary new
   colors, lighting, anatomy, backgrounds or collection frequencies.
7. Only after owner approval, validate final serving/storage URIs, image/hash
   bindings and live retrieval separately from placeholder JSON. No tokens,
   contracts, metadata publication, mint activation or marketplace acceptance
   are established by the present four development examples.

Open questions: higher-detail source availability, acceptable derivative quality,
per-source compressed size, measured RSS/CPU time, contact re-registration cost,
review throughput and release hosting. No precise schedule, throughput, cost or
5280-image feasibility claim is supported yet.
